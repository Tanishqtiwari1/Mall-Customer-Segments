    How it works

    A standard unsupervised-learning pipeline. The full analysis, with algorithm comparisons and a stability check, is in the project notebook.

    1. 1

      Explore

      200 customers: gender, age, income, spending score. No missing values. Income vs spending shows five visible groups.

    2. 2

      Scale

      Standardise income and spending to mean 0 and SD 1, so neither dominates the distance calculation.

    3. 3

      Choose k

      The elbow method and silhouette score both point to k = 5.

    4. 4

      Cluster

      K-Means with 10 initialisations. The result is identical across 20 random seeds.

    5. 5

      Profile and act

      Name each segment from its centre and attach a marketing strategy.

    Data: Mall Customer Segmentation (Kaggle). Model: scikit-learn K-Means, runs entirely in your browser.
